Functions run in containers that are stopped when idle, so the next request after a quiet spell pays for a container to be born again. This console measures that bill and works out the idle timeout your own traffic argues for.

Where a cold start goes

        

          Four numbers rather than one, because they belong to different
          people. Creating and starting the container are the daemon's work
          and no handler can change them. Waiting for ready is the interpreter
          booting and importing your code, and that part is yours.
        

        

        

      

      

        
Two populations, not one average

        

          Served requests, counted into log-spaced buckets. The gap between
          the two groups is the whole subject: a percentile taken across both
          mostly reports how often a container had to start.
